    Build

    Modeling the fin can

    The fin can is the 900 mm aft section. It carries the motor mount, the parachute and the fin set, so it was modeled first.

    Build

    Printing the fins

    Three trapezoidal fins, printed in PETG at 100% infill and 4 mm thick.

    Structure

    The bulkhead

    A bulkhead in the lower body holds the fin can and body together, and still lets ejection gas through.

    Retains

    It locks the fin can to the body so the two sections stay together.

    Inspired by Virgo

    The design follows the bulkhead from MIT's Virgo rocket.

    Ported

    Unlike the Virgo bulkhead, it is open to ejection gases, so they pass through to the recovery system.

    Systems

    Inside the airframe

    Forward bay

    624 mm filament-wound fiberglass. Avionics sled, altimeter, switch and battery.

    Motor mount

    380 mm fiberglass tube with an AeroPack RA38P retainer.

    Recovery

    36 in ripstop nylon parachute, Nomex protector and shock cord.

    Nose cone

    Fiberglass ogive, 162.6 mm long.

    Rail buttons

    Two Delrin buttons.

    Motor

    Cesaroni 634I540-16A, 38 mm.

    Finish

    Red and black

    The final paint scheme is red and black.
